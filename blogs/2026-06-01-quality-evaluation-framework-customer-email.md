Microsoft's Quality Evaluation Agent (QEA) in Dynamics 365 Customer Service now supports email evaluations in public preview, assessing outbound emails against predefined quality benchmarks. The framework evaluates emails written by humans or co-authored with AI, producing scores and coaching recommendations. This capability complements real-time governance policies to create a comprehensive cross-channel quality management solution.
