Microsoft's Customer Assist Agent combines generative AI with deterministic controls to evolve traditional IVR systems into more intelligent, conversational solutions in Dynamics 365 Contact Center. The platform enables natural customer interactions while preserving enterprise requirements like reliability and compliance, and allows organizations to modernize incrementally by integrating AI voice capabilities alongside existing workflows.
