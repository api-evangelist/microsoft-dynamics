Email Classification is now generally available in Dynamics 365 Customer Service, using AI to evaluate incoming emails before cases are created. By categorizing messages and filtering non-actionable ones through Automatic Record Creation rules, the feature helps service teams focus on genuine customer issues while reducing queue noise and improving productivity.
