Microsoft announced interoperability between Dynamics 365 Field Service and Project Operations, enabling organizations to unify service execution with financial tracking. The integration allows work orders to connect with projects for continuous visibility into costs, revenue, and profitability while streamlining the journey from estimates through invoicing and posting.
